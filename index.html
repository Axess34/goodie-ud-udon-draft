About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

Step in and you're in another world.

GOODIE is a hip-hop and R&B music bar on Thahan Road in the middle of Udon Thani. Our DJs dig deep — heavy beats, international hits and Thai tracks remixed the GOODIE way — so you can nod along all night.

No need to dress up. The vibe is a little street, a little raw and always friendly. Grab a drink, find your spot and let the music do the rest.
